# Interviews and compact review

Ask in short rounds. Before each question, check whether sources or earlier answers already settle it. Maintain question records with ID, topic, dependency, affected claims, intended respondent/role, priority, and state: open, answered, answered-by-evidence, deferred, or no-longer-relevant. Keep the reason and evidence for retiring a question. An answer can eliminate dependent questions only when it actually entails their answers in scope.

Prioritize strategic impact, consequence of error, conflicts blocking work, and questions that resolve many dependent uncertainties. For example, one analytics export may answer traffic, indexing, and monetization readiness; ask for that evidence source before requesting separate guesses. Do not ask users to restate clearly recorded decisions.

Use concrete cases when eliciting experience: the last difficult incident, what was known then, alternatives, decision cues, exceptions, and what would have changed the outcome. Record question and answer verbatim when available, speaker, role, time, context, and any limitations. Corrections become new evidence and durable constraints.

Separate review acts:

- Source expert confirms faithful representation of their account.
- Challenger checks exceptions, contrary evidence, and completeness.
- Accountable owner authorizes goals, strategy, interpretation, or policy.
- Curator checks clarity, provenance, and retrieval eligibility.

A solo owner may perform all human roles; record which act was actually performed. Never infer approval from silence, a general positive reaction, or structure approval.

## Packet

Propose a default budget of five decision items or roughly ten minutes, adjustable by the user. Group related claims around one meaningful decision without concealing individual claim versions. A bulk approval must identify its included items and versions. Present:

1. **Decisions needed:** strategic and consequential items first. For each: exact proposal, why now, source excerpts/links, alternatives or uncertainty, affected documents, and the specific review requested.
2. **Automatically incorporated:** a concise summary of machine-confirmed factual updates and links to their checks. This summary does not request or imply blanket human approval.
3. **Deferred:** count, oldest age, highest-priority remaining items, and link to the persistent backlog. Bring aging items forward to prevent starvation.

Use a stable packet ID and bind each review to the current claim/content version. Record explicit outcomes: approve, correct, reject, defer, or outside expertise. Revisions after approval require new review when meaningful. An approval of structure is recorded separately from approval of knowledge.

Interrupt active project work only for a conflict or missing decision that blocks that work. Otherwise collect it for the packet. When the user is unavailable, continue independent eligible work and checkpoint pending decisions; never treat elapsed time as authorization.
